7 Rounds for Tawaf & Saʿi  ·  One theme per circuit

How to use

Each round has a theme. Use rounds 1–7 for your 7 circuits of Tawaf, then restart from Round 1 for your 7 rounds of Saʿi. Each card contains 4–5 curated duas for that theme.
